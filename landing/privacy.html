Privacy

Updated October 17, 2026.

On this page

If you join the Sync Pro waitlist, we store your email, the date, the page language and the theme that was active. We use the email only to tell you when Sync Pro opens for testing. The data lives in a Supabase database; the page’s public key can add emails but cannot read the list.

The page sets no cookies of its own for tracking, runs no analytics and loads nothing from third parties: the font, images and scripts come from the same address. If you pick a language with the EN/PT switch, the page saves that choice in a small cookie so the next visit opens in the same language.

In the app

Without signing in, your projects stay in your browser’s storage. With an account (Google or email), a copy goes to Supabase, where each account reads only its own projects. Attachments sit in a private bucket and open through links that expire after an hour.

Your rights

To leave the list, correct or delete your data, reply to the email you receive or open an issue on GitHub. We handle requests under Brazil’s LGPD (Law 13.709/2018) and honor the same rights for visitors from other countries.
